WHAT A POD MEANS

A dedicated team.
A shared delivery responsibility.

A pod is a dedicated group of specialists accountable for an agreed delivery scope. Your product owner sets priorities; the Cosmonet delivery lead coordinates the skills, work and reviews.

01 / ALIGNED

Priorities stay with you.

You set the business priorities and acceptance criteria. The team works within your product roadmap, systems and decision-making process.

02 / CONNECTED

Skills work together.

Combine engineering, AI, data and quality expertise around the same backlog, with a delivery lead coordinating the work.

03 / CONTINUOUS

Knowledge stays accessible.

Build continuity through shared documentation, code reviews, operating guides and a defined plan for onboarding and transition.
