Website privacy notice · Updated October 29, 2026

What this website collects

The enquiry form collects your name, company, email address, selected service, optional business stage and project description, together with consent and submission timestamps. Campaign labels in a referring link may also be saved.

Why it is collected

The information is used to review and respond to your project enquiry and understand which services interest visitors. Please do not include passwords, financial account details or confidential client records.

Storage and access

Enquiries are stored through this website’s hosting and database services and are available to authorised site administrators. This website does not publish enquiry records or provide a public enquiry-search endpoint. Submission controls use a short-lived, hashed network identifier to limit automated abuse.

Website behaviour

This version does not add advertising pixels or marketing analytics. The hosting provider may process technical request and access information to operate and secure the site. Platform access controls may apply while this website is in private review.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact hello@cosmonet.ai with enquiries about your information, corrections or deletion. Include enough context to identify your enquiry without sending sensitive documents.
