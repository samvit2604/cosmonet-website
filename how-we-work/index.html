Defined scope.
Accountable delivery.

Agree the outcome, work in reviewable increments and prepare for operation. Our delivery process makes responsibilities, acceptance decisions and the work after launch explicit.

THE WORKING AGREEMENT

Agree how the work will be managed.

  • Scope, deliverables and acceptance criteria agreed before the build
  • Responsibilities, decision owners and a review cadence
  • Source access, documentation and handover terms
  • Change requests assessed for cost, timing and impact
  • Support coverage and third-party costs defined in writing

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

How much will a project cost?+

Cost depends on scope, integrations, data readiness and delivery requirements. We agree the work and commercial terms after an initial requirements conversation.

Can you work with our current systems?+

We assess your applications, APIs, data and access constraints before recommending a design. Integration feasibility is confirmed during discovery.

Can we start with a small engagement?+

Yes. A focused discovery, prototype or bounded workflow can be a practical first step before a wider build.

Who owns the source code and deliverables?+

Ownership, access, licensing and handover are defined in the engagement agreement. We make those terms explicit before work starts.

What happens after launch?+

We can scope support, maintenance, monitoring and further development. Coverage, response targets and responsibilities are confirmed in writing.
