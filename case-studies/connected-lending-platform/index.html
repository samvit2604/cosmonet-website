ILLUSTRATIVE SOLUTION · BANKING PLATFORMS

Connecting a lending platform to its operating environment.

An illustrative solution blueprint covering the business problem, proposed delivery and measures of success.

Discuss a similar challenge↗
IN THIS STORYThe challengeThe approachIntended outcomeWhat to measure

01 / THE CHALLENGE

The operational challenge.

A lender needs to connect a core lending platform with customer channels, reporting and downstream finance systems while preserving operational controls.

02 / THE APPROACH

The proposed delivery approach.

  1. Document lending workflows, product and accounting rules, approval responsibilities and downstream reporting needs.
  2. Specify interface contracts, field mappings, timing and reconciliation rules across customer channels, the lending platform and finance systems.
  3. Test normal and exception journeys, including rejected messages, retries and mismatched balances. Link defects to the affected business process.
  4. Rehearse data conversion and cutover, agree release criteria and prepare operating runbooks with issue ownership and escalation.

03 / INTENDED OUTCOME

The target operating model.

The intended result is a lending integration with traceable data flows, tested reconciliation and an operating team prepared for the transition.

04 / WHAT TO MEASURE

Define success before the build.

  • Reconciliation exceptions
  • Critical-journey test coverage
  • Integration failure and recovery rates
  • Open defects at the release decision

For a live engagement, agree baselines and targets before implementation.
