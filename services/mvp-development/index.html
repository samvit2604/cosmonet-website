THE BUSINESS PRIORITY

What should change
for your business?

Early products can absorb time and budget before the central assumption has been tested. We define one valuable user journey, separate essentials from later features and build a release that produces useful evidence.

01

A focused first investment

Put effort into the assumptions that affect the product decision.

MeasureValidated assumptions and scope changes
02

A complete user journey

Give early users a meaningful task they can finish.

MeasureActivation and journey completion
03

A better next-release decision

Use behaviour and feedback to select improvements.

MeasureRepeat usage and support themes

Success measures are agreed against your starting point and project scope before delivery.

WHAT WE DO

How we turn requirements
into working systems.

For startup founders, small businesses launching a new service and enterprise teams testing a product opportunity.

01

Product discovery & scope

Identify the target user, unmet need and commercial assumption. Define the first journey, acceptance criteria and features that can wait.

02

Prototypes & technical validation

Test usability with an interactive prototype and assess uncertain integrations or data requirements with a focused proof of concept.

03

MVP design & development

Build the essential web or mobile experience, backend and integrations. Include access controls, testing and the operating basics needed for a real launch.

04

Launch, feedback & iteration

Instrument the agreed product events, collect user feedback and review friction points. Turn the findings into a prioritised next-release backlog.

DELIVERABLES & HANDOVER

What your team receives.

Agree the deliverables and acceptance criteria before delivery begins. Keep the source, decisions and operating guidance needed to maintain the solution.

  • Product brief and prioritised release scope
  • Prototype and agreed first-release application
  • Analytics events and a validation plan
  • Source handover and next-release recommendations
