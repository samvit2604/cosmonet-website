WHAT WE DO

How we turn requirements
into working systems.

Choose a repeatable information or workflow problem, establish a baseline and develop an AI solution your team can evaluate and operate.

01

AI applications & copilots

Add search, summarisation, drafting and guided actions to customer portals and internal tools. Define the task, the available context and the review required for each output.

02

Knowledge assistants & retrieval

Connect approved documents and business records to assistants using Claude, OpenAI or an agreed model. Apply access rules, show sources and handle questions the evidence cannot answer.

03

Voice & conversational AI

Design call and chat flows for enquiries, qualification, scheduling and transfers. Connect business systems and agree consent, escalation and recording requirements before rollout.

04

Agents & tool integration

Connect models to approved APIs, databases and applications. Validate inputs and outputs, limit permissions and require approval where actions carry business consequences.

05

Evaluation & operational monitoring

Test representative tasks and failure cases. Track answer quality, completion, latency and cost, with a process for investigating errors and updating the solution.

06

Use-case strategy & team enablement

Compare AI with rules-based automation, prioritise opportunities and prepare a pilot. Train users and define who owns content, evaluations and operational changes.
