BUILDING YOUR TEAM

Recruitment questions,
answered.

Can you help us build a complete pod?+

Yes. We can scope complementary roles around your roadmap, including engineering, AI, data, quality and delivery leadership. Team size and assignments are agreed after the requirements discussion.

Can you recruit for one specialist role?+

Yes. Start with the specific capability and working arrangement you need. We define the role, assessment criteria and recruitment scope around that requirement.

Who sets the priorities for a dedicated pod?+

Your organisation sets the business direction and priorities. The dedicated Cosmonet team works within the agreed roadmap, responsibilities and review process.

What do you need from us to start?+

A short description of the work, skills required, desired working arrangement and timing is enough for the first conversation. We can help refine the brief from there.
